Why book a private session?

  • You are pregnant

    A gentle practice adapted to how you feel and to each stage of your pregnancy, with space to rest and breathe. Please check with your midwife or doctor before you start.

  • You are working with an injury

    Specific support for a sore back, knee, shoulder or hip, or while you recover from surgery. We modify poses and use props so you can keep moving safely, alongside your physio's advice.

  • You are on holiday in Ballina

    Keep your practice going while you visit the Northern Rivers. Book a time that fits around the beach and your plans.

  • You are new to yoga

    Learn the foundations at your own pace, with no one watching, so you walk into your first group class feeling confident.

  • You have a goal in mind

    Work towards something specific, like a pose you have been chasing, better flexibility or a short home practice you can do on your own.

  • The timetable does not suit you

    Find a time that works around your work, family or shifts, or simply enjoy a quiet hour of practice that is all yours.
