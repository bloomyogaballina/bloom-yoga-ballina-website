Meet Selin

Good yoga. A warm welcome. Familiar faces.

Selin is a yoga teacher and hypnotherapist with over a decade of practice, trained by some of the world's most respected teachers across India, Turkey, the US and Australia.

Selin's movement journey began as a dancer. Twenty years ago she walked into her first yoga class at a local gym and found something dance hadn't given her: a gentle, accepting practice that met her exactly as she was. It was love at first sight. She has never stopped practising since, and for more than 15 years she has kept a daily self-practice.

She brings a rare blend of intelligent movement, meditative awareness and deep presence to every class. Her flows are both uplifting and calming, and she has a gift for making every student feel truly seen.

Through her hypnotherapy practice and yoga teaching, Selin's passion is inner transformation. Helping people find stillness, grounding and a genuine connection to themselves.

Selin is bringing together the things she loves at Bloom: teaching yoga and welcoming people in. She wants the studio to feel friendly from the first visit, with good movement, familiar faces and room to be yourself.

Her Turkish heritage is woven through the little details, from the tulip in the logo to the little details around the studio. It is a personal part of the welcome.
