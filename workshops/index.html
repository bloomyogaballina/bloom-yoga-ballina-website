Workshops

Go deeper, one theme at a time.

Every fortnight, Selin runs a two-hour workshop that takes one part of the practice and explores it properly: breaking poses down, refining alignment and building towards each pose step by step. Workshops run on the first and third Sunday of each month, from 10:30 am to 12:30 pm.
